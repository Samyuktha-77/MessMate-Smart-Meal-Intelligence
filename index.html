THE PROJECT · 04

Behind MessWise.

A supervised learning prototype for university meal demand and food waste estimation.

PROJECT QUESTION

Can meal context help us prepare closer to what students will actually consume?

Two regression tasks are explored: predicting portions consumed and estimating food waste from planned preparation and pre-meal context. The workflow includes data preparation, chronological validation, model comparison, tuning and interpretation.

Technology stack

PythonScikit-learnPandasJoblibVercelHTML · CSS · JS

Validation design

Chronological split: 70% training, 15% validation and 15% final testing. This keeps the newest records held out for final evaluation.

Dataset transparency

The current dataset is synthetic and simulated for academic demonstration. It does not contain actual university mess records. Results must not be presented as proof of real-world waste reduction. Real operational use requires consented records, retraining and validation.
